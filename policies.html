Privacy Policy

When you create an account, this application stores your email address, the date your account was created, and the date and version of the policies you accepted. Your password is processed into a one-way password hash; the original password is not stored. Your schedules, uploaded files, folder names, and support messages are stored with your account. Files are limited to 20 MB each. The administrator can see account details, recent account activity, and messages sent to support, but not your password or private file contents.

Account and workspace records are kept in local files on the server in this starter version. Sign-in session data is held in server memory and is cleared when the server restarts.
