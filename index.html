Privacy center

Your schedule, profile, profile photo, and uploaded files are stored in the workspace database and associated with your account. Your password is stored as a one-way hash, never as plain text. Email verification and password reset links are sent via Resend when email delivery is configured. The administrator can view account activity and messages sent to support, but cannot see your password or private file contents. Deleting your account removes your workspace and profile data.
